Thesis: 'Preclinical discovery of biomarkers of response to a novel tyrosine kinase inhibitor using large-scale pharmacogenomic screens.'

  • Identified ovarian clear cell carcinoma as a consistently sensitive subtype using a generalised linear mixed model combining two independent viability screens (GDSC, Oncolines; 1000+ cell lines), then nominated and confirmed ARID1A loss as the associated biomarker across public and in-house datasets.

  • Validated the association experimentally by generating isogenic CRISPR-Cas9 knockout lines and profiling them by RNA-seq and RPPA; analysed the resulting data (Nextflow, DESeq2, limma, GSEA, STRINGdb) to establish the mechanism underlying sensitivity. Findings will inform patient stratification for upcoming clinical trials (manuscript in preparation).

  • Defined a bespoke drug-response metric to separate specific cytostatic drug activity from non-specific cytotoxicity in large-scale viability screens, embedded in a reproducible end-to-end concentration-response modelling pipeline in R (repo).

  • Designed and coordinated follow-up experiments with internal and external collaborators (including in vivo work).

  • Showed through cross-validated benchmarking (Python, scikit-learn, Linux HPC) that machine learning models trained on multi-omic data for response prediction offered no gain over a subtype baseline, redirecting discovery efforts.
